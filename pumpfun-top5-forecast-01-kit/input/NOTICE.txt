Where these marks come from

Each line of history.ndjson is one read of public Solana mainnet state by make.fun's recorder
(collector/pump-forecast in the make.fun repository): the pump.fun coins created by the pump program
6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P at or after t0 and quoted in SOL, each priced from its own accounts
read through getMultipleAccounts from a Solana mainnet RPC endpoint (the recording's record.json names its scheme
and host), ranked by market cap. The data is public chain data, the same from any mainnet RPC node, so a row checks
against any of them; a coin's name and symbol are the ones its CreateEvent carried on chain.

A coin in pump.fun's mayhem mode (is_mayhem_mode on its bonding curve or pool) is ranked only once its pool prices
it: on its bonding curve, and while it awaits migration, its price stands on virtual reserves with almost no SOL
behind them, so no mark ranks it there (and neither does the answer). Once it graduates, its pool row ranks like any
other.

A row's market cap is in lamports, on the coin's fixed total supply S in raw units (6 decimals), floored: S = 10^15
for an ordinary coin (pump.fun's 1B), and S = 2 * 10^15 for a mayhem-mode coin (its row's "mayhem" true: pump.fun's
1B plus the 1B its Mayhem Mode agent mints at creation), held at 2B even after the agent burns what it has not sold
24 hours in. S is fixed by the kind of coin, never read from the mint's supply:
  curve      floor(curve.virtual_quote_reserves * S / curve.virtual_token_reserves)
  pool       floor((quote vault amount + Pool.virtual_quote_reserves) * S / base vault amount), on the coin's
             canonical PumpSwap pool (PDA ["pool", u16 LE 0, PDA(["pool-authority", mint], pump program), mint, WSOL]
             under pAMMBay6oceH9fJKBRHGP5D4bD4sWpmSwMn52FMfXEA) and its two vaults
  migrating  a complete curve whose pool is not created yet: floor(quote * S / base), base the curve's own base
             token account's amount, quote curve.real_quote_reserves + curve.post_complete_quote_in -
             Global.pool_migration_fee (the reserves the migration deposits)

A row's "slot" is the context slot of the read it was priced from: the accounts as they stood at that slot. Public
RPC serves only current account state, so a row is checked against the chain through the transactions that set
those accounts, which it keeps:
  - list the transactions on the row's account (its bonding curve for a curve or migrating row, its pool's vaults
    for a pool row) with getSignaturesForAddress, and take the last one that landed at or before the row's slot;
  - a curve row: that transaction's pump TradeEvent carries the curve's reserves after the trade
    (virtual_sol_reserves, virtual_token_reserves): the curve formula on them is the row's cap;
  - a pool row: that transaction's meta.postTokenBalances hold both vaults' amounts after it, and the Pool
    account's virtual_quote_reserves (a signed i128 at byte 245) completes the pool formula;
  - a migrating row: the completing transaction's TradeEvent and post-balances give the curve's reserves and its
    base account's amount (pump-fun/pump-public-docs, SYNTHETIC_MIGRATION.md).
